High-redshift quasars and their small- and large-scale environments

In 2023 and 2024, I led the analysis of a Cycle 1 JWST program to detect the host galaxies of five quasars in the Epoch of Reionization, above z=5. After performing careful image decomposition to disentangle the faint emission of the host galaxy from the bright quasar, we found that all five galaxies were significantly undermassive relative to their central supermassive black holes.
